# Review cancellation and refund requests

Every way out of a booking comes from one rule, `bookingExit` in `components/bookings/booking-exit.ts`, built from /terms §8 (`lib/legal/terms.ts`, id `cancellation`). The booking popup, `/bookings/detail` and `/bookings/refund?bookingId=<owned booking UUID>` all read it, so a booking shows at most one way out and one line about its money:

- A held slot (PENDING_PAYMENT) whose receipt reads unpaid or is missing, before the start: the plain cancel in the popup, nothing about money. Until its receipt answers it is offered nothing, as every booking is: API develop settles the charge before it confirms the booking, and a list cached in another tab can still say held, so a held slot may already be paid. One whose receipt reads paid follows the paid rules below, and the popup reads the booking again once so its header and main button can follow the server (see the second decision).
- Paid, booked, 24 hours or more before the start: cancel and get the whole charge back.
- Paid, booked, inside 24 hours: cancel and get 50% of the service fee back; the platform fee is kept.
- From the start (or once in progress, completed or marked a no-show) until 7 days after the end: no cancel; report a problem and request a refund. Sending never changes the booking.
- After that: the refund window is closed.
- Cancelled and paid with no request: claim the refund owed with one press (the whole charge when the advisor or the team cancelled). An advisee's own cancel at or after the start is no cancellation under §8 (nobody may cancel a session that has begun; the API's cancel checks no time, and the old refund form cancelled ended sessions before filing): it reads as a session that began, open to a report for the whole charge until 7 days after the end, and its request, if any, is a report under review.
- A request on record: its status only, never a second request. An open cancellation reads "ส่งเรื่องคืนเงินแล้ว", which the team confirms within 14 business days; only a reported problem is reviewed, by the team, within 14 business days.
- Booked (or under way, or over) without a paid receipt: on API develop only a paid charge or a reschedule's replacement leaves PENDING_PAYMENT, so its money is somewhere the page cannot see. With no receipt it is a replacement, paid on the booking it replaced ("ชำระไว้กับการจองเดิม…"). With a receipt that reads pending or failed it was paid on another charge, a declined card paid on a retry (third follow-up), and the popup shows "การจองนี้ยืนยันแล้ว แต่ใบเสร็จของการจองนี้ในระบบไม่ใช่ใบที่ชำระ…" instead of that receipt. Either way it offers no cancel, no move and no price, only support (`paidElsewhere`). A cancelled booking without a paid receipt says no payment was found and offers support; only a held slot says it was not paid.
- Wherever support is the way on, the button reads "ส่งอีเมลถึงฝ่ายช่วยเหลือ" with an envelope, since it opens an email rather than a step in the app, and for a booking still on, the line beside it names the §8 line the email does not stop (`runningDeadline`): the full refund until S − 24 h, the half until S, then a report until E + 7 days.
- Rescheduling is offered only 24 hours or more before the start (`rescheduleWay`): a held slot moves in place over the advisor's slots. A paid one is not moved (see the decision below); where the move would be, the popup says what another time costs, beside the cancel it names, the deadline left to the line above it: "ต้องการเวลาอื่น ยกเลิกแล้วจองใหม่ นัดใหม่ชำระแยก ส่วนเงินนัดนี้คืนเมื่อทีมงานยืนยัน".
- A money read that fails names what failed, what was paid or the refund requests, and a 403 says the account cannot do it, in its own words. On API develop every advisor-role account gets that 403 on its own refund requests (fourth follow-up).
- The popup reads the clock when it opens, not when the list behind it loaded, so its money line and the move follow the 24-hour line as it stands.

Cancel-and-refund cancels first and checks it, then opens the request. The rule is read again with the clock at the press, so a page left open across the 24-hour line or the start sends nothing and moves to the offer that holds now. A lost response is read back before any second POST. If the cancel POST fails and the booking reads cancelled anyway (someone else's cancel answered with a 409, or this press's reply lost), nothing is filed: the booking is read again and its claim follows who cancelled and when. If the cancel stood and the request did not, the page lands on the claim with an alert, so the money is never stranded. A cancel refused because the booking changed reads the booking again; an answer that may hide a cancel offers "ตรวจสอบสถานะ" before anything is sent again, and the line under the card then says what the check found. A lapsed session asks to sign in again and come back; "ลองอีกครั้ง" appears only where pressing again can help (a network failure, a timeout or a server fault), and a refusal offers support instead, in the popup's money line, on the page's load states and as the claim's button. Before the red button the page says the cancel is at once and the refund waits for the team to confirm it within 14 business days. A request the API refuses with a 409 because one is already open is taken as that request. If it carries the reason this press sent, it is this press's own (an earlier press whose answer was lost, pressed again), and the report's files are uploaded to it; otherwise the page says one was open and that this press's details and files were not added. A lost answer, a timeout or a server fault is adopted without a word. Nothing is offered while the invoice or the requests are still loading, and a screen that opens on a cached answer reads it again. When a read again fails, the page keeps what it last showed with one line about it; the full-page failure is only for a read that never answered.

Case approval is distinct from a payment refund: approving a case only changes its status and moves no money yet. The card refund through the payment provider is API #86, which also has to refund part of a charge before the 50% shown here can be paid back as shown. Until then an approved request reads "อนุมัติแล้ว · รอคืนเงิน", nothing before an invoice the API marks refunded says the money is on its way, and only that invoice reads "คืนเงินแล้ว". The 14 business days are the time to decide, not to pay. The page never promises a date the money arrives; invoice status remains authoritative.

## Decision needed: the free move of a paid booking

/terms §8 grants one free move at least 24 hours ahead. On API develop that move cannot be told from the advisee's own early cancel (first follow-up), so a moved original would read as owed its whole charge while the new session goes ahead. The screens default to withholding the in-app move for a paid booking and offering, by the same deadline, the cancel for the whole charge followed by a new booking.

That default has a real cost, and since every real booking is paid, §8's free move is gone for all of them. The new booking is a second full charge, paid now. The first booking's refund waits for the team to confirm it, up to 14 business days, and approval moves no money yet (API #86), so for that time the advisee has paid twice. The popup says so ("นัดใหม่ชำระแยก ส่วนเงินนัดนี้คืนเมื่อทีมงานยืนยัน"). The alternative is to restore the in-app move and accept that a moved original reads as owed its whole charge until the API marks it. The first follow-up removes the need for either.

## Decision needed: the popup header of a held slot that reads paid

The popup's header and main button stay as they were (the brief keeps them out of this change): a PENDING_PAYMENT booking reads "รอชำระเงิน" with a blue "ชำระเงิน" to the card checkout. When its receipt already reads paid (the `settleCharge` follow-up, or a list read before paying in another tab), the money line says "ชำระแล้ว" and the one way out is the cancel with its refund, so the card shows two statuses and two opposite actions. The popup now reads the booking again once when that happens, which settles a stale list. If the server itself still says PENDING_PAYMENT, the header and the pay button stay wrong until it confirms. Letting the header and main button follow a paid receipt is the one change this would need outside the brief's scope.

## API follow-ups

The screens hold these rules; the API does not check them yet, so another client (or a request crafted by hand) can still do what the screens will not offer.

- `POST /bookings/:id/reschedule` (`bookings.service.ts` `reschedule`) cancels the original as the advisee and marks it no other way, and it does not link the replacement booking to the invoice (`service_appointments_on_service_invoices`). So the original reads exactly like the advisee's own early cancel, owed its whole charge, while the new session goes ahead. Linking the replacement alone would not cure it: the invoice's session is its earliest linked booking (`firstAppointments` orders by start time), so a move to a later time keeps the session on the original. The API has to mark the original, for example a `replacedById` on the cancelled booking, or a session that skips cancelled bookings. Until it does, the screens withhold a paid booking's move and read a booked appointment with no receipt as paid on the one it replaced. This is the one to fix first.
- No staff route can move or cancel a booking: there is no admin bookings controller, `reschedule` and the advisee's `cancel` go through `findForAdvisee`, and the advisor's through `findForAdvisor`. Every support path on these screens (a booking paid on the one it replaced, a refused refund request, a refused money read) ends at the support mailbox with nothing in the system behind it. The team needs a staff route to move, cancel and refund a booking before those paths can be more than a mailbox.
- `findForBooking` (`payment.repository.ts`) takes any invoice linked to the booking, `.limit(1)` with no order, and `createBookingInvoice` (`payment.service.ts`) leaves an earlier FAILED invoice linked when the advisee pays again (a declined card, a coupon changed at checkout). So a paid booking can hand back its failed charge. It must prefer the paid (HELD_IN_ESCROW or RELEASED) invoice, then the newest. Until it does, a booked appointment whose receipt reads pending or failed goes to support.
- The advisor role holds no `refund` statement (`auth.config.ts`, `advisorStatements`), while an advisor-role account can book. So an advisee approved as an advisor later, or any advisor who books, gets a 403 on `GET /refunds/me` and `POST /refunds` for their own booking, and the screens can only send them to support. The advisor role needs `refund: permissions.refund.selfSubmit`.
- `settleCharge` (`payment.service.ts`) writes HELD_IN_ESCROW and only then calls `confirmPayment`, outside one transaction, so for a moment (or for good, if the second write fails) a paid booking is still PENDING_PAYMENT. The screens follow the paid receipt, offer a held slot nothing until its receipt answers, and read the booking again once when the receipt reads paid; only the API can make the two writes one.
- An advisor's or the team's cancel of a paid booking notifies nobody: in `bookings.service.ts` only `confirmPayment` sends a notification. The advisee learns the whole charge is owed back ("รับเงินคืน ฿X") only by opening that booking. A notification on every cancel of a paid booking, naming the refund, closes that.
- The same route checks neither the 24-hour line nor the one free move of /terms §8. A booking 5 hours out can be moved to next week and then cancelled for the whole charge instead of half; the popup now hides the move inside 24 hours, but only the API can enforce it.
- `POST /bookings/:id/cancel` (`cancel`) checks no time, so a booking still BOOKED past its start time can still be cancelled, and the old refund form did exactly that before filing every report. The screens read an advisee's cancel at or after the start as a session that began (a report for the whole charge), never as a late cancel at half. The share is judged later from `cancelledAt`, which lands a network trip after the press, so a press seconds before the 24-hour line can be recorded on the half side of it.
- `POST /refunds` takes the reason as text; the share and the amount are not checked against `cancelledAt`, and nothing closes the report window 7 days after the end. A request can't be edited once open, so details typed in a second tab after another tab opened one are not added (the page says so).
- API #86: approving moves no money, and the refund it adds is always the whole charge.

## Local flow

Start the API at http://localhost:3200 and frontend at http://localhost:3001 with the disposable `advisory_proof` database in `advisory-booking-proof-db`, local Mailpit and private SeaweedFS. An advisee needs their own paid invoice linked to a booking. Open the booking and use its one way out (**ยกเลิกและรับเงินคืน** before the start, **ขอคืนเงิน** after it); for a report pick what happened, optionally add details and evidence, then send. Reload to verify the case, cancellation and files. An admin can inspect the attachment in `/admin/refunds/review?id=<case UUID>` and rule on the request; going back to the booking and into its refund row shows the decision without a reload.

The opt-in browser proof arranges synthetic paid fixtures only in that named local database and deletes them afterward; it never charges a card. Set `BOOKING_LIVE_PROOF=1` and `REFUND_EVIDENCE_CLEANUP_SCRIPT` to the API checkout's `test/refund-evidence-cleanup.cjs`. That helper requires explicit `REFUND_PROOF_DATABASE_URL`, `REFUND_PROOF_S3_ENDPOINT`, `REFUND_PROOF_S3_ACCESS_KEY`, `REFUND_PROOF_S3_SECRET_KEY` and `REFUND_PROOF_S3_BUCKET`, and refuses remote hosts or another database name. Run:

```sh
pnpm exec playwright test tests/e2e/refund-request-live.spec.ts
```

The two scenarios cover desktop 1440px approval and phone 390px rejection, persistence after refresh, byte-for-byte downloads, unsigned403, admin attachment review and the unchanged paid invoice. The desktop scenario also drives Swagger's evidence-list operation, proving200 for the owned case and404 for a missing one. Browser console errors are checked. Database cleanup still runs if object cleanup fails.

## Design and limits

The page is laid out as the receipt of the refund: inside the account frame at the bookings section's width, one card with the status line, the amount in large type and the facts on the left and the conditions, the report form or the progress on the right, the one main button under it, and one way back to the booking at the top instead of a breadcrumb. The two cells end within a row of each other at 1440: a half refund's box sits beside its amount with the rule running under both, on the head's columns. A report's form runs under a head that spans the card, in the order it is filled in (reasons beside the details, then evidence), and a short trail (a refusal, a refund with no request) runs under the head the same way. The report reasons are the grounds §8 names. The mocked browser proof is `tests/e2e/refund-flow.spec.ts` at 1440 and 390.

JPEG, PNG, WebP and PDF evidence is optional, at most five files per case and 50 MB each. Identical retries reuse the stored file. Private links expire after five minutes; admin review refreshes links before expiry. The requester's files are available to their own session and admins with refund-read permission.
